VisitBrief

Half of what you meant to tell the doctor evaporates in the waiting room. VisitBrief turns thirty seconds of daily logging into a one-screen brief: what's wrong, when it started, whether it's getting worse, and the questions you actually wanted answered.

  • Symptom timeline with onset day count and worst day per symptom
  • Trend detection: recent 3 days vs the 3 before - getting worse, stable, or improving
  • Ranked brief so the most severe symptom leads the conversation
  • Question list that survives until the appointment, copyable as one text block
  • No account, no cloud - everything stays in your browser
